A mark for Arunananda, built from what's actually his

The circle-and-teardrop icon on the site now is a placeholder, it could belong to almost any wellness channel. These two directions come from two facts that are specific to him: what his name means, and where his teaching comes from. Pick one, ask for changes, or send us back to the drawing board, your call.

Current mark, live on the site today, shown for comparison only.
Concept one

Udaya

उदय · “rising”, the word for sunrise

Aruna, the root of his name, is the charioteer of the dawn in Hindu myth, the reddish light that rides ahead of the sun every morning. Udaya renders that as one continuous ring in first-light color, open at a single gap. The gap does two jobs at once: it reads as a horizon where light is breaking through, and as the near-touching thumb and finger of Gyan Mudra, the seal his own videos teach more than any other. One shape, two truths about him.

Concept two

Girivas

गिरिवास · “mountain-dweller”

Before Ananda Ashram, there were years alone in the Himalayas. Girivas is plainer and quieter: a single mountain line in the site's own dusk teal, with one warm point of light resting at the summit, the same gradient as Udaya, held in reserve rather than spent everywhere. It reads less like a teaching mark and more like a record: this is where it came from.

Which to run with

Both use the same warm gradient for their one point of light, so they're a real family, not two unrelated pitches. The choice is really about tone.

Choose Udaya if the site should feel like an invitation

Warmer, more present, closer to what a viewer feels watching a mudra taught step by step. Works hardest in the nav and on the WhatsApp CTA.

Choose Girivas if the site should feel like a record

Quieter, more monastic, leans on his years of solitary practice rather than the teaching itself. Works hardest in the footer and on the About section.

Or send us back

Tell us what's off, tighter ring, different gap, a third direction entirely, and we'll revise before anything touches the live site.
